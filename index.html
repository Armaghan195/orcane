Ownership

The problem

Generic software wasn't built for you. You pay for that every week.

Off-the-shelf tools force your team to change how they already work. You pay a licence for a hundred features you will never touch, while the one thing your business actually depends on is the thing the platform cannot do.

So the gaps get filled by hand. Spreadsheets, re-keyed data, WhatsApp approvals, month-end reconciliation done by eye. None of it appears on an invoice, and all of it comes out of your team's week.

→ Orcane builds the system around your workflow instead — your terminology, your process, your scale. Nobody retrains, because nothing changes but the manual work disappearing.

Software should adapt to the business, not the other way around.

That principle decides every project we take on. Whether we are digitising a distributor's decades-old ledger or building a white-label SaaS platform from scratch, we start by learning your real vocabulary and real constraints — then engineer around them.

  1. 001 / Full-stack under one roof

    Backend, frontend, mobile, AI, and infrastructure handled by one accountable team. No stitching together freelancers, no handoffs between vendors, no gaps for a problem to hide in.

  2. 002 / Built around your business

    We adopt your exact terminology and workflow from day one. Your team reads its own vocabulary on screen, so there is nothing to translate and nothing to retrain.

  3. 003 / AI-native engineering

    Machine learning, LLMs, and automation are core skills here, not a buzzword bolted onto a sales pitch. We ask where automation can remove manual work before we design anything.

  4. 004 / Direct access to engineers

    You talk to the people building your product, every time — not a relay of account managers. Weekly check-ins, honest trade-offs, and no surprises at delivery.

  5. 005 / Engineering discipline

    Clean architecture, tested code, and documentation are non-negotiable, not optional extras. Every system ships with docs, so you are never locked out of your own product.

  6. 006 / We don't disappear after launch

    Launch day is the beginning. Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, and iteration keep the system running as your business grows — with flexible models that scale with you.

Decades of ledgers on paper and disconnected spreadsheets, with no way to reconcile dispatch against billing. We built a dual-portal ERP — a daily dispatch view and an asynchronous finance and khata view — automatically reconciled against each other, using the client's exact vocabulary throughout.

Key features

  • Dual-portal architecture A daily godown and dispatch portal for physical stock movement, and an async finance portal for billing, sharing one database.

  • Dual pricing & tax layers An FBR-compliant invoicing layer alongside an internal true P&L layer, tracking real margins and carriage costs.
  • Client-exact terminology Every label — khata, MDA, godown — mirrors the client's own business vocabulary, so nobody needed retraining.

We shipped one Next.js application running the marketing site, an SEO-optimised blog, and an internal command centre with first-party analytics and a full lead pipeline.

How we work

A process built to
keep you informed.

Every project runs the same five stages. Weekly check-ins, direct access to the engineers building your product, and no surprises at delivery.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map your workflow, vocabulary, and constraints through direct conversation — how the work really happens, not how a process document says it should. Nothing is assumed and nothing is proposed before we understand it.

    • Workflow & requirements mapping
    • Written scope and constraints

  2. 02

    Design

    We wireframe the key flows and validate the plan before any production code is written. You see how the system will look and behave — and approve it — while changes are still cheap.

    • Wireframes and user flows
    • System architecture and stack

  3. 03

    Develop

    We build in focused sprints with visible, testable progress at every stage. You see real, working software at each milestone — never a big reveal at the end — and priorities can shift as feedback comes in.

    • Short sprint cycles
    • Working demos and weekly check-ins

  4. 04

    Test

    We verify against real data and edge cases, not just clean demo scenarios. Functional, regression, performance, and security checks all run before anything reaches you, so we catch the bugs first.

    • Automated and manual QA
    • Security and performance checks

  5. 05

    Deploy & support

    We handle the full deployment — domains, cloud hosting, CI/CD, and go-live — then stay engaged for monitoring and iteration. You receive the full source code and documentation, so you are never dependent on any one person.

    • Production deployment & handover
    • Ongoing maintenance and monitoring

Every project comes with

  • Security by design Authentication, data isolation, and access control built in from day one.
  • Tested before shipping Automated and manual testing against real-world data and edge cases.
  • Full documentation You are never dependent on one person to understand your own system.
  • Direct communication You talk to the engineers building your product, not a relay of managers.
  • Post-launch support Ongoing maintenance and monitoring, so nothing breaks silently.
  • Transparent timelines Real progress, real dates, honest trade-offs — never inflated promises.
